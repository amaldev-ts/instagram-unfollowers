How to Get Your Instagram Data ZIP

1

Open Instagram Settings

Go to your Instagram profile → tap ☰ Menu → Settings and Privacy

2

Accounts Center

Tap Accounts Center → Your Information and Permissions

3

Download Your Information

Tap Download Your Information → Download or Transfer Information

4

Select Your Account

Choose your Instagram account and tap Next

5

Select Connections

Choose Some of Your Information → Select Connections → tap Next

6

⚠️ Choose JSON Format (Important!)

Select Download to Device → Format: JSON (must be JSON, not HTML) → Date range: All Time → tap Create Files

7

Download & Upload

Wait for Instagram to prepare your file. Download the ZIP and upload it below!

Important: Use JSON Format

When downloading your Instagram data, please select JSON format (not HTML) for best results and accurate analysis.

Drag & Drop your ZIP file here

or click to browse files
