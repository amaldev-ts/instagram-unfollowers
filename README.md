# 📸 Instagram Unfollowers Finder

A professional web application to analyze your Instagram data and discover who doesn't follow you back, your fans, blocked accounts, and more.

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue)
![Made with](https://img.shields.io/badge/made%20with-HTML%20CSS%20JS-orange)

## 🚀 Features

- **Drag & Drop** ZIP file upload
- **Unfollowers Detection** — Find who you follow but doesn't follow you back
- **Fans Detection** — Find who follows you but you don't follow back
- **Complete Analysis** of:
  - Followers & Following
  - Pending Follow Requests
  - Blocked Profiles
  - Recently Unfollowed Profiles
  - Recent Follow Requests
  - Restricted Profiles
- **Search** through usernames
- **Mark/Select** users with visual strikethrough
- **Click to visit** Instagram profiles directly
- **Download** results as PDF, JSON, or HTML
- **100% Client-side** — No data leaves your browser
- Professional dark theme UI

## 📋 How to Get Your Instagram Data

1. Open Instagram → **Settings and Privacy**
2. **Accounts Center** → **Your Information and Permissions**
3. **Download Your Information** → **Download or Transfer Information**
4. Select your account → **Next**
5. Choose **Some of Your Information** → Select **Connections** → **Next**
6. Set format to **JSON**, Date range: **All Time** → **Create Files**
7. Download the ZIP when ready and upload it here!
