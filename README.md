# 📸 Instagram Unfollowers Finder

A professional web application to analyze your Instagram data and discover who doesn't follow you back, your fans, mutuals, blocked users, close friends, and more.

🔗 **Live Demo**: https://amaldev-ts.github.io/instagram-unfollowers/

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 **Find Unfollowers** — People you follow but don't follow you back
- ❤️ **Find Fans** — People who follow you but you don't follow back
- 🤝 **Find Mutuals** — People who follow you and you follow back
- 📊 **Complete Analysis** — Followers, Following, Pending, Blocked, Restricted, Close Friends, Hide Story From
- 🎨 **Dark/Light Theme** — Toggle between themes
- 📥 **4 Download Formats** — PDF, JSON, HTML, CSV
- 🔒 **100% Private** — All data processed in your browser
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Works on mobile, tablet, desktop
- ✅ **Mark/Track** — Mark users you've already checked

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Get Your Instagram Data**: Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Download Your Information → Connections → **JSON format**
2. **Upload ZIP** to the app
3. **Click Analyze** and explore!
